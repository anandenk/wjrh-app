About the station

Freeform by design

Broadcasting from Hogg Hall since 1946, WJRH gives live DJs full control of what reaches the air. The station is entirely student-managed, all-volunteer, and built around programming that would be difficult to hear anywhere else.
WJRH is a federally licensed, noncommercial educational station owned by the Lafayette College Board of Trustees. It never airs commercials and offers hands-on experience in music, broadcasting, audio production, engineering, acoustics, podcasting, and station management.

From Radio Lafayette to WJRH

Photograph courtesy of Skillman Library Special Collections
Radio Lafayette began on the third floor of Hogg Hall in 1946. The call letters WJRH arrived in May 1947, honoring James Renwick Hogg, whose name the station’s home still carries.
1946
Radio Lafayette is established in Hogg Hall.
October 1, 1947
The first official broadcast, “Songs at Seven,” airs from 7 to 11 PM.
1948–1949
Regular sports coverage begins, followed by quiz shows and talk programming.
2011
WJRH begins streaming online at WJRH.org.
Today
A recording studio, podcasting, student and community DJs, and a proudly freeform signal.
