# Recent-track placeholder artwork

These images are decorative placeholders, not actual cover art for broadcast tracks or station shows in the WJRH prototype. They provide visual rhythm for the recent-track list using original abstract geometric artwork designed around the station's cream, maroon, radio, and vinyl aesthetic.

Corresponding fallback artwork for the native mobile app is bundled at `apps/mobile/assets/images/demo-cover-1.jpg` through `demo-cover-5.jpg` (see the mobile [demo guide](../../docs/mobile-app/archive-demo.md#covers)).

1. `recent-cover-01.jpg` — Concentric vinyl record with maroon center label and warm gold accent ring
2. `recent-cover-02.jpg` — Bauhaus broadcast sunburst with radiant signal waves on a rich maroon field
3. `recent-cover-03.jpg` — Modernist constructivist frequency grid and sound architecture blocks on cream
4. `recent-cover-04.jpg` — Harmonic analog waveforms and resonant oscillation ribbons on a navy gradient
5. `recent-cover-05.jpg` — Minimalist turntable with offset charcoal vinyl and brass tone arm geometry on terracotta
