Every screen, sheet and stream state in the prototype. Two tabs, one persistent player.

NowDefault tab. Play button pinned at the bottom, one tap to audio.
1 · First launch
One-time. Names the station and what's on right now; "Listen live" starts audio immediately.
2 · Now, idle
Show card, track card, Coming up, Last hour. Player in the thumb zone.
3 · Now, playing
Pause glyph, "LIVE · PLAYING", small signal bars animate. Output route button on the right.
4 · Track sheet
Tap any track. Title + artist only, played on, Find this track, Copy title. No screen push.
ScheduleMini player persists on every non-Now screen.
5 · Schedule
Day picker (today marked), past slots dimmed, ON AIR chip, reminder badge with slot time. Times in ET.
6 · Show detail
Host, description, airs, Remind me, last playlist. Live shows also get "Listen now" (acts in place).
7 · Reminder permission
First Remind me tap. Explains the single notification before the OS prompt.
AboutBehind the "i" button. Not a tab.
8 · About
Station blurb, Host a show, Request a song, Support, socials. External rows say OPEN ↗.
9 · Host a show sheet
Three steps and an email field. Submits to the station's list; confirmation card replaces the form.
Stream statesSame Now screen under different conditions.
10 · Connecting
Spinner in the play button, "CONNECTING…". Also shown on every press.
11 · Reconnecting
Stream dropped mid-play. Banner, spinner, live dot stops; retries automatically.
12 · No track info
Laptop DJ or empty metadata. Track card muted and not tappable; audio still plays.
13 · Stream down
Play disabled, banner names the next live show, Remind me sets that reminder.
14 · No network
Last loaded content stays visible; play disabled; Retry.
15 · Schedule at 9 PM
Past/live/upcoming derive from the clock, never list position. Same screen as 5, later in the day.
